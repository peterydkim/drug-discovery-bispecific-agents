## AGENT 01 — TARGET IDENTIFICATION REPORT

**Composite Score: 91/100 — Highest Priority Target Pair**

### PD-1 (PDCD1)
- Gene: PDCD1. Location: 2q37.3. Type I transmembrane protein, Ig superfamily.
- Function: Immune checkpoint receptor. Suppresses T-cell function upon PD-L1/PD-L2 binding.
- Cancer relevance: Clinically validated across 15+ tumor types. 4 FDA-approved anti-PD-1 mAbs (pembrolizumab $26.3B 2024 sales).
- Open Targets: Top-ranked across NSCLC, melanoma, RCC, HNSCC, CRC. >500 active trials.
- Safety: G3-4 irAEs ~20%. Manageable with corticosteroids.
- HPA tissue expression: Restricted to immune compartments (activated T cells, Tregs, B cells, NK cells).

### VEGF-A (VEGFA)
- Gene: VEGFA. Location: 6p21.1. Secreted growth factor, disulfide-linked homodimer.
- Function: Master regulator of angiogenesis. Also immunosuppressive: inhibits DC maturation, promotes Treg/MDSC, upregulates PD-L1 on tumor endothelium.
- Cancer relevance: Bevacizumab approved 2004. PD-1+VEGF combos FDA-approved in HCC, RCC, endometrial, NSCLC.
- Open Targets: Top-ranked (0.492 for CRC). >725 Phase 3 trials.
- Safety: Hypertension (8-15% G3-4), proteinuria (2-5%), bleeding. Bevacizumab contraindicated in squamous NSCLC.
- HPA: Widely expressed; high in lung, colorectal, renal, breast, liver cancers.

### Why PD-1 + VEGF as a Pair?
1. **Cooperative binding:** VEGF dimers cross-link ivonescimab, creating >18-fold PD-1 affinity enhancement — unique to bispecific format, not achievable with co-administered mAbs.
2. **Dual mechanism:** PD-1 blockade reactivates exhausted CD8+ T cells. VEGF blockade normalizes tumor vasculature and reverses immunosuppression.
3. **Phase 3 validation:** HARMONi-2: HR 0.51 vs pembrolizumab. First drug to beat Keytruda head-to-head.
4. **Regulatory precedent:** PD-1 + VEGF combinations FDA-approved in 4 indications.

**Source:** Open Targets, DisGeNET, GWAS Catalog, UniProt, HPA, PubMed (PMID 16917489, 11815711).

## AGENT 03 — BISPECIFIC DESIGN REPORT

### Ivonescimab (AK112/SMT112) Architecture

**Format:** Humanized IgG1-scFv tetravalent (2+2) bispecific antibody.
- Anti-VEGF: two conventional Fab arms at N-terminus of IgG1 (bivalent)
- Anti-PD-1: two scFv domains fused to C-terminus of each heavy chain (bivalent)
- Fc: Human IgG1 with L234A/L235A (DM) double mutation — Fc-silent, abolishing FcγRI/IIIa binding, ADCC, CDC, ADCP
- MW: ~201.1 kDa
- Half-life: 6-10 days (single dose), ~9.85 days (steady state)
- CAS: 2428381-53-5
- Originator: Akeso Biopharma (China). Licensee: Summit Therapeutics (global ex-China).
- Regulatory: NMPA approved May 2024. BLA filed FDA (2026).

### Cooperative Binding — Format-Dependent Differentiator

The tetravalent 2+2 format is not a valency preference — it is a mechanistic requirement. VEGF dimers cross-link ivonescimab molecules via the anti-VEGF Fab arms, clustering anti-PD-1 scFvs to create avidity effects. A bivalent 1+1 format cannot do this. A two-mAb combination cannot physically couple the targets.

Mechanism: VEGF dimers form soluble complexes with ivonescimab (SEC-HPLC confirmed). These complexes exhibit slower PD-1 dissociation kinetics (koff). The slower koff drives >18-fold apparent KD improvement. PD-1 binding reciprocally enhances VEGF capture >4-fold. Mass photometry confirmed 1:2:2 (ivonescimab:VEGF:PD-1) ternary complexes.

### Format Comparison

| Feature | Ivonescimab (Tetravalent 2+2) | Bivalent 1+1 | Bev + Pem (mAb combo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valency | 2 PD-1 + 2 VEGF | 1 PD-1 + 1 VEGF | 2 PD-1 + 2 VEGF (separate) |
| Cooperative binding | Yes (>18×, >4×) | None | None |
| VEGF-dimer bridging | Yes | No | No |
| TME retention | Enhanced | Standard IgG PK | Independent |
| MW | ~200 kDa | ~150 kDa | ~300 kDa (combined) |
| Half-life | 6-10 days | ~21 days | 20-21 days |
| Manufacturing | High complexity (4 chains, scFv folding) | Moderate (CrossMAb) | Simple (two lines) |
| Clinical status | Approved (China), Ph3 global | No Ph3 for PD-1/VEGF | Approved globally |

### Structural Data Gaps
- No crystal or cryo-EM structure of ivonescimab deposited in PDB
- No structures of ivonescimab + VEGF or ivonescimab + PD-1
- The relative orientation of Fab arms and C-terminal scFvs is unknown
- This prevents atomic-level understanding of the cooperative binding interface

### Developability
- Expression: CHO-K1. scFv domains risk aggregation (moderate concern).
- Immunogenicity: Low-moderate. Humanized sequences; scFv linker may be neo-epitope.
- FcRn recycling preserved (L234A/L235A in lower hinge, not CH2-CH3).
- Safety: HARMONi-2 G3+ TRAE 29.4%. Proteinuria 31.5%. Hypertension 15.7%. Manageable.

**Sources:** SITC 2023 Abstract #1194 (JITC); AACR 2024 Abstract B123; iScience 2025 (PMID 39898030); PDB (5JXE, 5GGS, 5WT9, 4ZQK, 1BJ1).

### Comparison to Parental mAbs

| mAb | Target | Consensus KD |
|---|---|---|
| Pembrolizumab | PD-1 | 3.9 nM (SPR, Brown 2020). 29 pM value measures avidity, not true affinity. |
| Nivolumab | PD-1 | 3.06 nM (EMA). 7.2 nM (SPR, Brown 2020). |
| Bevacizumab | VEGF-A | 1.1 nM (EMA). |
| Penpulimab | PD-1 | 0.59 nM (SPR). Reference comparator in iScience 2025. |

### Why Lower Baseline PD-1 Affinity is Intentional

1. **TME-selective targeting:** Weak baseline PD-1 binding spares normal tissues. In VEGF-rich tumors, cooperative binding drives affinity >18-fold.
2. **Reduced systemic irAE risk:** Minimizes PD-1 blockade in VEGF-low healthy tissues.
3. **Avidity engineering:** VEGF-dimer "daisy chaining" creates kinetically trapped PD-1 binding.
4. **Manufacturability:** Moderate baseline affinity preserves expression and stability.

Quantitative context:
```
Baseline PD-1 blockade:   IC₅₀ = 1.216 nM  (2.7× weaker than nivolumab)
With VEGF cooperativity:  ~18-fold → effective IC₅₀ ≈ 0.068 nM
                         exceeds nivolumab (0.449 nM) by ~6.6×
```

### T-cell Activation (PBMC/Raji-PD-L1 Co-culture)

- IFN-γ secretion: Significantly enhanced with VEGF co-incubation (p<0.01-0.001 vs ivonescimab alone)
- IL-2 secretion: Same pattern — VEGF-dependent enhancement
- Superior to pembrolizumab + bevacizumab combination at equimolar concentrations
- VEGF-enhanced T-cell activation is format-dependent: requires tetravalent bispecific, not replicable with mAb combo

### Key Functional Mechanisms

1. **VEGF-enhanced PD-1 blockade:** Cooperative binding transforms a moderate-affinity blocker (IC₅₀ 22.54 nM) into a potent one (IC₅₀ 3.29 nM)
2. **VEGF-enhanced PD-1 internalization:** Receptor downregulation from T-cell surface — sustains T-cell activity
3. **Fc-silent advantage:** No ADCC/CDC/ADCP. Pure checkpoint/angiogenesis blockade. T cells are not depleted.

## AGENT 06 — IN VIVO & CLINICAL DATA REPORT

### Preclinical In Vivo
- HCC827 (EGFR-mutant NSCLC) and U87MG (glioblastoma) hPBMC-humanized mouse models: dose-dependent tumor growth inhibition
- Superior to pembrolizumab + bevacizumab combination at equivalent doses
- Tumor vasculature normalization confirmed by immunohistochemistry

### Phase 3 Clinical Results

#### HARMONi-2 (NCT05499390): Ivonescimab vs Pembrolizumab, 1L PD-L1+ NSCLC
- **PFS:** 11.14 mo vs 5.82 mo (HR 0.51, p<0.0001)
- **ORR:** 50.0% vs 38.5%
- **G3+ TRAE:** 29.4% vs 15.6%
- **Historic:** First randomized Phase 3 to beat pembrolizumab head-to-head
- Source: Zhou et al., Lancet 2025

#### HARMONi-A (AK112-301): Ivonescimab + Chemo vs Placebo + Chemo, EGFRm NSCLC Post-TKI
- **OS:** 16.8 mo vs 14.1 mo (HR 0.74, p=0.019) — final analysis at 32.5 months median follow-up
- **PFS:** HR 0.46, p<0.001
- **Status:** Basis for NMPA approval (May 2024). BLA filed FDA (2026).

#### HARMONi-6 (ASCO 2026 Plenary): Ivonescimab + Chemo vs PD-1 + Chemo, 1L Squamous NSCLC
- **OS:** 34% reduction in death risk (HR 0.66). mOS 27.9 mo vs 23.7 mo.
- **Significance:** Bevacizumab is contraindicated in squamous NSCLC. Ivonescimab's cooperative binding enables safe VEGF blockade here. Zero TRAE deaths in squamous subgroup.
- First China-only dataset presented at ASCO Plenary

#### HARMONi-3 (NCT05899660): Global Phase 3, Ivonescimab + Chemo vs Pembrolizumab + Chemo, 1L Non-Squamous NSCLC
- **Status:** Enrolling globally. Interim PFS missed in April 2026 update. Final analysis critical.

#### HARMONi-7 (NCT06084455): Global Phase 3, Ivonescimab vs Pembrolizumab, 1L PD-L1 High NSCLC
- **Status:** Enrolling globally.

#### AK112-206 (ASCO 2026): Phase 2 Global, Ivonescimab + mFOLFOX6, 1L mCRC
- **ORR:** 70.8% (vs historical 45-55% with bevacizumab + FOLFOX)
- **Status:** HARMONi-GI3 Phase 3 enrolling (NCT07228832)

### Why Ivonescimab Succeeded

1. **Cooperative binding is real and format-requiring.** The >18-fold PD-1 affinity boost in the TME cannot be replicated by co-administering separate mAbs. This is the core differentiator.
2. **Squamous NSCLC breakthrough.** Bevacizumab is contraindicated in squamous NSCLC due to fatal pulmonary hemorrhage. Ivonescimab's TME-directed VEGF blockade makes it safe in this population. 34% OS benefit vs PD-1 + chemo.
3. **Single-agent chemo-free regimen.** HARMONi-2 showed ivonescimab monotherapy beats pembrolizumab monotherapy. This opens the door to chemo-sparing regimens.
4. **First-mover advantage.** First PD-1/VEGF bispecific to market. NMPA approved. BLA filed FDA.

## GO / NO-GO RECOMMENDATION

**VERDICT: GO — STRONG RECOMMENDATION** (Confidence: 9/10)

PD-1 × VEGF is the strongest bispecific antibody target pair in oncology by clinical evidence. The cooperative binding mechanism creates a pharmacological advantage over co-administration that is format-dependent and not replicable by separate mAbs. HARMONi-2 (PFS HR 0.51) and HARMONi-6 (OS HR 0.66) provide Phase 3 proof-of-concept unmatched by any other bispecific pair.

### Key Risks
- **HARMONi-3 interim PFS missed** (April 2026). Final analysis critical.
- **Regional generalizability.** China-only pivotal data. Global Phase 3 must replicate.
- **9+ competitors.** $30B+ committed. Merck entering with Keytruda franchise protection.
- **OS data still maturing.** HARMONi-2 median follow-up 8.7 months.

### Recommended Indications
1. 1L PD-L1+ NSCLC (HARMONi-2 validated, chemo-free)
2. 1L squamous NSCLC (HARMONi-6 validated, unique safety advantage)
3. EGFRm NSCLC post-TKI (HARMONi-A validated, approved)
4. 1L mCRC (AK112-206 ORR 70.8%, Phase 3 enrolling)
5. HCC (atezo+bev precedent)
6. RCC (pembro+axitinib precedent)
