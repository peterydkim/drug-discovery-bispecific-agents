# Drug Discovery Glossary — Bispecific Antibodies

## Immuno Oncology Targets

**PD-1 (Programmed Death 1):** Receptor on activated T cells. When PD-1 binds PD-L1 or PD-L2 on tumor cells, T-cell function is suppressed. Blocking PD-1 restores anti tumor immunity. FDA approved drugs: pembrolizumab (Keytruda), nivolumab (Opdivo), cemiplimab (Libtayo).

**PD-L1 (PD-1 Ligand 1):** Expressed on tumor cells and antigen presenting cells. Binds PD-1 to suppress T-cell activity. FDA approved drugs: atezolizumab (Tecentriq), durvalumab (Imfinzi), avelumab (Bavencio).

**LAG-3 (Lymphocyte Activation Gene 3):** Expressed together with PD-1 on exhausted T cells. Binds MHC-II with higher affinity than CD4, transmitting inhibitory signals. Also binds FGL1, galectin-3, LSECtin. FDA approved: relatlimab (in Opdualag, combined with nivolumab).

**CTLA-4 (Cytotoxic T-Lymphocyte Associated Protein 4):** Competes with CD28 for B7 ligands (CD80/CD86). Higher affinity for CD80 than CD28. Approved drug: ipilimumab (Yervoy).

## Bispecific Antibody Formats

**IgG-like (1+1, bivalent):** One binding site per target. Knobs-into-holes (KiH) or CrossMab technology to prevent chain mispairing. Best PK, lowest aggregation risk. Example: tobemstomig (Roche).

**Tetravalent (2+2):** Two binding sites per target. Higher avidity on dual-positive cells. Formats: DART (Dual-Affinity Re-Targeting), FIT-Ig (Fabs-In-Tandem), DVD-Ig (Dual Variable Domain). Example: clinical DART-format bispecifics.

**Fragment-based:** BiTE (bispecific T-cell engager), tandem diabody. No Fc region, shorter half-life, typically for T-cell redirection (e.g., blinatumomab).

## Binding Assays

**SPR (Surface Plasmon Resonance):** Real-time label-free binding kinetics. Measures association rate (ka), dissociation rate (kd), and equilibrium constant (KD). Gold standard for antibody affinity.

**BLI (Bio-Layer Interferometry):** Label-free binding, higher throughput than SPR. Octet systems. Slightly less sensitive than SPR.

**ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay):** Plate-based binding quantification. Lower resolution than SPR/BLI but widely used for screening.

**KD (dissociation constant):** Lower = tighter binding. Units: M. Typical mAb KD range: pM (10⁻¹²) to nM (10⁻⁹).

**Avidity:** Combined binding strength of multivalent interaction. Tetravalent bispecifics have avidity advantages on dual-positive cells.

## Functional Assays

**Reporter gene assay:** Cells engineered to express luciferase upon pathway activation. Used to measure immune pathway blockade potency (e.g., PD-1/PD-L1 blockade bioassay).

**Mixed lymphocyte reaction (MLR):** Co-culture of allogeneic T cells and dendritic cells. Measures functional immune activation (cytokine release, proliferation).

**T-cell exhaustion reversal:** Tumor infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) assays measuring IFN-γ, IL-2, TNF-α restoration after immune pathway blockade.

**Bridging assay:** Measures bispecific's ability to simultaneously bind both targets on the same or adjacent cells. Key for avidity-driven design.

**ADCC/CDC:** Antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity / complement-dependent cytotoxicity. Fc region design determines effector function.

## Clinical Terms

**IND (Investigational New Drug):** FDA filing to begin human trials. 30-day review period.

**Phase I:** Safety + dosing, 20-80 patients.

**Phase II:** Preliminary efficacy + safety, 100-300 patients.

**Phase III:** Confirmatory efficacy vs standard of care, 300-3000+ patients.

**ORR:** Objective Response Rate. % patients with complete or partial response.

**PFS:** Progression-Free Survival.

**OS:** Overall Survival.

**Grade 3-4 TRAEs:** Treatment-Related Adverse Events, Grade 3 (severe) or 4 (life-threatening).

**CBR-24:** Clinical Benefit Rate at 24 weeks. Used in early phase immunotherapy trials.

| Toxicity | Lower (~15-20% G3-4) | Higher (~50-60% G3-4) | VEGF-AEs (HTN, bleeding) |
| Mechanism | T-cell exhaustion reversal | Broad T-cell activation + Treg depletion | Dual immune + anti-angiogenic |
