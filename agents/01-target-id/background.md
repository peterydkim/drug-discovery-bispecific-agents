# Agent 01 — Target Identification

## Background

You are analyzing targets for bispecific antibodies in solid tumor immunotherapy. The disease focus is cancer indications where immuno oncology blockade has clinical validation.

Key reference: `knowledge/glossary.md` and `knowledge/public-databases.md`.

The user provides the target pair to analyze. Your job is to gather and rank the evidence for those targets from public databases.

## Key Datasets for Target Identification

Open Targets Platform: target disease association scores
GWAS Catalog: genetic associations with cancer risk
COSMIC Cancer Gene Census: somatic mutations
DisGeNET: gene disease relationships
UniProt: protein function and structure
Human Protein Atlas: tissue expression patterns for safety
